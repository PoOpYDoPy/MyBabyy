# Kanishka & Tanmay Music Player — Songs Folder

Place your audio files directly into this folder:
- 1.mp3
- 2.mp3
- 3.mp3
- 4.mp3
- 5.mp3
...
(Supported formats: .mp3, .m4a, .wav, .ogg, .aac, .flac)

These correspond to:
- 1.mp3 -> Track 1
- 2.mp3 -> Track 2
- 3.mp3 -> Track 3
- 4.mp3 -> Track 4
- 5.mp3 -> Track 5
...

You can also tell the AI your song titles (e.g., "Song 1 is Tum Se Hi by Mohit Chauhan"), and the song titles will be customized in the player for you!
